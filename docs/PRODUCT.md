# Understudy: product and surface brief

## Who visits, and what they need in 30 seconds
- **First reader:** someone deciding where AI should go in an operational process. They skim. In 30 seconds they need:
  1. the question, in one line
  2. the answer, as one or two numbers they can repeat
  3. why to trust it: real data, results held back from the builder, failures shown
- **Second reader:** someone checking the work. They need the method, the rules written before the runs, the misses, and a way to open the cases behind any number.

## The one-line question
Which invoices can an AI pass straight to payment without a person, and how do you prove it before you let it?

## What the page must make obvious
1. **The work:** where people spend their time in one real purchase-to-pay process (251,734 order lines).
2. **The audition:** three understudies, a plain rule, Claude judging each invoice, and Claude writing a policy, tested against what actually happened.
3. **The bar:** a 99% lower bound on straight-through calls being right, and why passing every invoice fails it.
4. **Earned autonomy:** a week-by-week replay where autonomy is granted per case type only once outcomes prove it, and taken back when it slips.
5. **The rubber stamp:** the same replay gated by reviewer agreement promotes sooner and lets more wrong payments through. This part is a labelled simulation.
6. **Cost at volume:** cost and time per 1,000 invoices for each understudy.
7. **Honesty:** every rule was written before its run, every replaced rule stays on the record, and the limits are stated.

## Tone
Plain, exact, unhurried. A findings report, not a product launch. Numbers are drawn to scale. Nothing on the page is typed by hand; every figure comes from the data.

## Surfaces
- One long findings page that leads with the answer, then the evidence.
- A case drawer: click any figure to see the invoices behind it, with ID, case type, the call made and the outcome.
- A methods section, collapsed by default, holding the rules and their dates.

## Out of scope
Sign-in, uploads, live recalculation, analytics.
