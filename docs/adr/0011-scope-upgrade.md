# 0011. Upgrade to a findings report with three contenders, a replay and an agreement-gate comparison

## Decision
Understudy becomes a findings report rather than a one-time test. It has three contenders, an earned-autonomy replay and a simulated comparison with an agreement-based gate. Everything built before this (parser, analysis, recount, candidates, locked held-out set) is kept unchanged.

## Alternatives
- Finish the two-helper plan as it was.
- Switch to a different question.

## Why
The two-helper plan asked a language model to label table rows, which is a weak use of it. It also measured once, at sample size, and left the build's strongest idea, that reviewers rubber-stamp, as advice on the page. Whoever decides where AI goes is accountable for risk and cost at volume. The upgrade measures both, and shows autonomy being earned and lost over time.

## Consequences
- More work in the same deadline. The cut line: if the build is behind at 2026-11-17 12:00, the agreement-gate section is dropped and the page says so.
- The results may be modest: the plain rule may win, or few case types may pass. That is reported as found.
