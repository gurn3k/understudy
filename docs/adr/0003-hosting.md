# 0003. Hosted on its own subdomain

## Decision
The page lives on its own subdomain of the owner's domain. Deploying needs the owner's approval, with every check closed.

## Alternatives
A path under the portfolio site; a default Vercel URL.

## Why
A clean, shareable link, and no sub-path hosting issues.

## Consequences
The DNS and hosting setup is a manual owner step on Nov 18.
